About

From Doctor to Medical Virtual Assistant

I am a Medical Doctor with over 5 years of clinical experience across diverse healthcare settings. I am passionate about helping healthcare professionals stay organized, efficient, and focused on what matters most — their patients.

As a Medical Virtual Assistant, I support healthcare practices with appointment scheduling, patient communication, administrative tasks, medical documentation, and general virtual assistance. With my medical background, I understand the importance of accuracy, confidentiality, empathy, and professionalism when working with patients and healthcare teams.

I am trained in EHR/EMR systems including eClinicalWorks, SimplePractice, TherapyNotes, and DrChrono, with practical experience in patient chart management, insurance eligibility and benefits verification, referrals, and prior authorizations.

Let me help you spend less time on administrative tasks and more time caring for your patients. ❤️
